Satchel membership cards that are wallets

Every card in this club is an NFT and a real NEAR account, for example 7.<club>. Anyone can send NEAR to it from any wallet. Ticket sales pay into every card's account. Whoever holds the card controls that money. Give or sell the card and the money and the members-only access go with it; the old holder keeps nothing.
